## Looking for

- Backend or full-stack development roles, TypeScript or Java.
- Teams that own a product, rather than short consulting assignments.
- Remote work or an office within commuting distance of Exampleville.

## Rules it out

- Roles that are mainly people management with no hands-on work.
- Roles requiring a security clearance.
- On-site work more than three days a week outside Exampleville.

## Weigh, but don't decide on

- Seniority words in the title: judge by the responsibilities described.
- Technologies listed as "nice to have".
- Salary, when the ad gives it.
