## Promising

- Titles naming backend, full-stack or test automation work.
- Employers that build their own product.
- Remote work, or an office in Exampleville.

## Not promising

- Titles that are mainly sales, people management or support.
- An office more than two hours from Exampleville, with no remote option.

## Don't decide on

- Seniority words in the title.
- An unknown company or location.
