## Voice

- Plain and direct. Short sentences, active voice, no superlatives.
- First person, but most sentences start with the work, not with "I".
- One concrete example per claim, taken from a `facts` result.

## Cover letter

- At most one page: an opening line naming the role, two or three paragraphs
  matching the ad's needs to earlier work, and a short close.
- The motivation paragraph is always a `[[ASK:qN]]` question.
- Finnish letters use "Hei" and sign off with "Ystävällisin terveisin".
- English letters use "Hello" and sign off with "Kind regards".

## CV

- Keep the template's Markdown headings and its section order. Move the most
  relevant role's bullets first and drop bullets the ad gives no reason to
  keep.
- Keep every employer and date in the template, even when shortened to one
  line.

## Naming

- Refer to earlier employers by the name they had at the time.
- Name projects only as the `facts` results name them.
