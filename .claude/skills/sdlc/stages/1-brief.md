# Stage 1 — Brief

Write `brief/<slug>.md` from `brief/TEMPLATE.md` by interviewing the user —
Problem, What done looks like, Approach, Out of scope, Open questions — one
question at a time.

Write what they actually said. Leave a section thin, or delete it, rather than
inventing requirements to fill it. A brief that answers questions the user
never considered is a plan's worth of assumptions wearing a brief's clothes.

Commit it. No approval step follows; the commit is the handoff — and it ends
the stage, so hand off to a fresh session rather than opening the plan here.

Next: Stage 2 (Plan), in a fresh session, in plan mode.
